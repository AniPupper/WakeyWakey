          How streaks work

          A morning adds to your streak when you hit both goals:

          • Up on time. Tap "I'm up" within 2 minutes of the alarm. Awake before it? Tap "I'm already up" in night mode. That counts too.
          • On your own. Answer "Yes, on my own" in the morning check-in.

          Phone-down time doesn't affect your streak. It's there to help you see your routine: green minutes mean your phone went down before bedtime, red minutes mean after.

          Days with no alarm don't break your streak. Forgot night mode? Use "Log a night" at the top to add it, and it still counts.

          Luna changes with the real moon phase, and glows once your streak reaches a week.

          Settings

          Wake-up sound

          How loud the alarm gets. Let go of the slider to hear it. This works on top of your phone's own volume, so keep that turned up.

            Every chime starts a little softer and builds to your alarm volume over the first minute. While you're testing Wakey Wakey, keep a backup alarm set in the Clock app.

            Testing

            Runs the whole morning: night mode, the alarm, "I'm up", the check-in and the celebration. Nothing is logged and your streak doesn't change.

            Data

            Removes your wake-ups, phone-down times and streak, for example after testing. Your schedule and settings stay.
